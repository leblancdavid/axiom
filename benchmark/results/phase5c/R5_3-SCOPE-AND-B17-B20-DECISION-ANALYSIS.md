# Phase 5C — R5.3 scope and B17–B20 acceptance decision analysis

**Status: analysis / recommendation for explicit authorization, not a protocol amendment or freeze.** R5.2.2 is the authoritative corrected post-B16 oracle. R5.3 is unfrozen; B17 is unexposed; B18–B20 have not been executed. This record does not change a frozen requirement, oracle, checkpoint, classification or application. It proposes replacing the *exhaustive R5.3 gate* with a narrower, independently authorized prospective acceptance-composition gate; no change to that gate is authorized by writing this record.

## Decision in brief

**Recommend C, targeted lineage with prospective first-class assertions (the useful part of B), against the executable R5.2.2 parent.** Exhaustive canonicalization of every B01–B16 independently rejectable path is not a necessary condition for valid B17–B20 execution. It would offer a stronger *global oracle-equivalence audit*, but it does not by itself make the B17 replacements safe. Conversely, merely writing new B17 assertions and skipping conflicting old methods (B alone) repeats the B11 preservation failure. The minimum defensible gate is exact, state-relative disposition of *affected active carriers*, explicit original and replacement semantics/preconditions for each changed observation, verified retention of every other assertion in any replaced carrier, and regression against the untouched frozen executable parent. This is a recommendation, **not yet a proof** that a replacement runner or B17 case satisfies it.

The additional risk that exhaustive R5.3 could eliminate is **an unknown, independently rejectable historical assertion path being omitted, altered or mis-preconditioned by an earlier whole-method supersession, even when all currently active R5.2.2 tests pass**. A complete source/helper/branch/runtime-to-root correspondence and semantic comparison could discover an omission analogous to the B11/B01 loss *outside the targeted future changes*. This is a stronger claim than “the currently frozen suites pass.” It would not prove the frozen cases exhaust the natural-language requirements, cover unexecuted states, or ensure future B17–B20 replacements are correctly written. Existing freezes, audits and repairs substantially reduce but do not logically eliminate that global historical risk. It should be tracked honestly, not relabeled zero simply because targeted work passes.

**A** is warranted as a separate historical-oracle research objective if the required scientific claim is global B01–B16 executable-semantic equivalence, or if targeted auditing reveals pervasive omissions that defeat the bounded gate. It is **not logically required** to test later frozen requests against a preserved R5.2.2 parent. Do not assert the stronger A claim under C. **B** is an essential prospective authoring discipline but not an adequate stand-alone transition. **D** is not recommended: a CLI-wide actor injection could erase B17's `actor_required` check if applied to the new cases, and a broad user-result normalizer could hide required roles.

## B17 concrete affected surface

`benchmark/requirements/B17.md` changes two independent historical dimensions: **A**, task mutations require a real `--actor` (including the existing `migrate` command, even on a zero-count migration, per `B17-AFFECTED-METHOD-AUDIT.md:9-16`); **B**, user result objects contain `role`. It additionally introduces actor existence/permission rules and error/no-write behavior, which need *new* B17 cases. Read-only calls and `create-user` do not gain a task actor. An authorized actor supplied to a formerly failing mutation must preserve its prior validation error and no-write assertion; a missing actor is tested separately, not substituted for that older error. Task result shape must not change. `migrate` counts, storage/schema and atomicity checks remain. Distinguish B17's `migrate` actor rule from B18's separately worded audit-entry operations; do not infer a new B18 migration audit entry.

The exact *historical semantic carriers to inspect and possibly replace* are the **active** method IDs in the two tables at `B17-AFFECTED-METHOD-AUDIT.md:52-118` (the table's `R.`, `Cnn.`, `R4.`, `R5.` expansion at lines 20-46 specifies full IDs):

- **B01–B16:** 33 active methods, **all** A-affected by at least one task mutation (including `migrate` in migration-only methods). These include the two baseline migration methods; B04/B06/B07/B08 migration-only methods; 24 active B16-R5 replacement methods (including B11/R4 descendant carriers); and three B16 case methods. Exactly **four** active methods also have B: `R5.Owner.test_registered_trim_reject_and_exact_owner_listing` (two `create-user` returns) and the three `C16.RequiredOwnership` methods (their asserted `list-users`/`create-user` results). See audit rows 77 and 91-97 for which calls assert shape; do not invent a B supersession for the unasserted `create-user known` result or invalid-user error. The built-in `system` result becomes ADMIN; newly registered default users become USER.
- **{B01,B04}:** seven active originals, all A-affected, none B-affected (no active B16 user-result assertion). The two B02 methods are prerequisite-skipped, not B17 supersessions. The full history's 28 earlier supersession skips stay with their **existing** B11/R4/R5 edges; do not re-skip an inactive original. On the early history the baseline lifecycle/B01 priority/B04 source originals are live, whereas on the full history their B11/R4 and then R5 descendants carry surviving requirements. The R5.2 profile dictionary by itself cannot resolve that collision (`B17-R5_2-COMPOSER-LIMITATION-HALT.md`, `R5_3-ACCEPTANCE-STATE-AUDIT.md`).
- R5.2.2's **additive** baseline/B01 restoration carriers must also be checked before a B17 transition, even though the B17 method audit counts 33 active R5.2 base-suite methods: on B11-achieved history there are two additional active methods carrying five repaired groups, including the exact three-task B01 precondition. They contain creation/completion calls and require actor adaptation if B17 activates. Do not treat the 33-method table as an exhaustive inventory of the corrected R5.2.2 executable suite or lose its five roots. The actual selected active suite, not a stale method-count assumption, is the gate.

Thus C is **targeted by semantic dimension and active carrier, not by a small number of methods**: with B17's broad actor requirement most currently applicable methods need adaptation. The saving versus A is that unchanged observation paths inside those methods can be preserved/replayed and independently audited for retention without constructing a global canonical root for every helper invocation, runtime event, or unrelated rejection path. For any rewritten method, a table of preserved/changed/added assertions with their setup and phase is necessary; a high-level list of “unrelated assertions” or a green result is not sufficient. Reuse exact original method bodies where possible with a narrowly scoped actor adapter; explicitly adapt role equalities and corrected repair carriers; independently inspect adapters/rewrites including call envelopes, errors and no-write checks. The audit's proposed 33+7 map is a strong *starting inventory*, not a finished B17 oracle.

B17 roles presuppose a persisted user registry in `B17.md`; Lykoi has a recorded B16 multiple-state/cross-entity capability gap (`B16-R5_2-RESULT.md`). Before a future B17 classification, decide the dependency from requirement semantics under the frozen blocked-by-gap rules, **not** from track name, request number or predicted outcome. If B17 is not achieved on a history, R5.2.2's applicable oracle, including its previous skips and corrections, remains in force. If achieved, conditional B17 acceptance activates only on that independently classified history. The prospective oracle must still be specified for both valid prior histories before exposure; do not assume that a dependency determination removes the need to handle conditional histories in the protocol.

## Minimum authorized design to evaluate *before* B17 exposure

1. **Parent and state:** hash-pin the unchanged R5.2.2 executable boundary and reconstruct the *effective* active/superseded/prerequisite-skipped suite, including R4/R5 overlay and R5.2.1/R5.2.2 repair carriers, solely from achieved history and pinned prior artifacts. Require exact loaded-ID and source-hash agreement, one applicable carrier per **targeted historical assertion** (without duplicate activation) and no duplicate second skip. Retain earlier supersession chains instead of overwriting them. This is a bounded legacy bridge, not a global semantic inventory.
2. **Prospective assertions:** before either track sees a request, give each new B17–B20 semantic assertion a stable ID and explicit operation, state/setup (including achieved-field applicability), observation phase, expected success/error/value/persistence and supersession/dependency relationship. Link a changed historical assertion to its pinned source site, original preconditions and active lineage; list retained observations for every affected method, including calls hidden in helpers and repairs. Records must say whether a new requirement adds a check or truly replaces an older one. Generate/derive independent backend-neutral executable cases where practicable, but keep the frozen parent executable as the authority for untouched legacy behavior.
3. **Transition and runner:** bind changed assertions to **active** carrier IDs, never merely to an original frozen ID already skipped. An origin not achieved is inapplicable; unsuccessful later requests cannot activate skips. For each selected method, require an exact pinned successor and a complete original-to-successor disposition (changed, retained, intentionally removed only with explicit requirement authority); fail on absent, extra active, duplicated or drifted methods/assertions. Prefer byte-identical replay of old bodies plus narrowly limited, checked adapters; prove unchanged argument/result/persistence checks still execute with equivalent relevant preconditions. A replacement's passing test alone does not prove this. Keep the previously frozen source and suite immutable.
4. **Independent gates:** review the affected inventory and replacement comparisons *before* request prediction/implementation; freeze a versioned, track-neutral amendment/case/fragment/map and both independently derived target suites/hashes. Rebuild and run the pre-activation parent on fresh, independent B16 restores with inventory and internal/external checks; test both alternative histories, conflict, omission, repair-carrier and fail-closed fixtures. Only after explicit authorization/freeze can the usual preflight → prediction → isolated implementation → applicable accumulated external/internal regression → classification → checkpoint/independent restore sequence resume. Recheck preservation at each B18–B20 freeze; stop for any unexplained affected-path discrepancy rather than treating it as a gap or silently waiving a test.

This gate is stricter than B's “write new records and preserve the rest somehow,” and narrower than A's “all historical rejection paths must be canonicalized.” For B18 audit history, B19 recurrence/completion and B20 project permissions (`requirements/B18.md`–`B20.md`), enumerate affected active assertions and preconditions *per request* and any previously composed B17+ assertions. B18's rejected/no-entry and atomicity, B19's completion/successor/audit-entry effects, and B20's ownership/project permission interactions each require their own new assertions; do not preselect a whole old method for skipping merely because it exercises `complete` or `create`. Authorship, coverage and classifications remain future work.

## Answers and authorization boundary

1. **Is exhaustive R5.3 necessary?** No, not for the narrower claim of a fair, cumulative B17–B20 comparison against the currently corrected historical executable boundary, *provided* the targeted gate above is satisfied. It remains necessary if claiming global historical canonical equivalence, which this proposal does not claim.
2. **Minimum representation?** Stable new assertion IDs with exact semantics/preconditions and supersession edges; pinned effective active method/repair-carrier state for each achieved history; exact site/observation/precondition descriptions for historical assertions *actually changed*; retention proof for all other assertions inside selected replacement methods; unselected parent tests left executable and byte-identical.
3. **Retain R5.3 work?** Preserve all existing R5.3 files, worksheets, inventories, lineage audits, traces, fixtures, tooling and open diagnostics as separately labelled prospective evidence. Reuse the effective-disposition reconstruction, R4/R5 lineage checks, corrected B01 precondition lesson, actor/user site inventory and deterministic trace observer where they answer targeted questions. The pure `acceptance_state_r5_3.py` transition is a prototype, not a frozen/complete runner.
4. **May remain unfinished?** Global disposition of the 187+21 candidates, global mapping of 1,416+275 unchecked semantic events, full rejection-path completeness and a zero-unexplained-difference certificate, **unless** a specific B17–B20 affected assertion needs such work or targeted auditing uncovers a broader defect. Keep their status visibly open; do not infer that unresolved diagnostics are proven failures or proven harmless.
5. **Avoid B11 defects?** Before freezing each later request, map changed assertions by exact precondition and observation, follow the active carrier through B11/R4/R5/R5.2.2 and earlier later-request edges, explicitly preserve/replay every other check in any replaced method, and test the original-versus-replacement scenarios independently on both achieved histories. The corrected B01 pending-NORMAL example is the minimum standard for precondition fidelity. Never substitute method counts or green regressions for that audit.
6. **Next suite architecture?** Make structured semantic requirements/assertion identities and supersession/dependency lineage **authoritative first**, then derive executable backend-neutral tests, fixtures and traceability from that source before either agent's exposure. Keep independent executable checks and checkpointed results. Retire the legacy Python-test reverse-engineering bridge instead of treating exhaustive R5.3 machinery as the reusable foundation for C01–C20-style suites.

**Request explicit authorization** for a separately versioned *targeted/prospective* protocol design and verification gate before any B17 freeze/exposure. If this approach cannot demonstrate exact carrier retention (notably the R5.2.2 repair carriers or helper/error paths), stop and broaden the targeted audit or reconsider A; this analysis is neither authorization to skip that gate nor evidence of B17 success.
