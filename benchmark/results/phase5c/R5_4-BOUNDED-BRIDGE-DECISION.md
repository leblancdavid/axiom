# Phase 5C R5.4 — bounded semantic-requirement bridge decision

**Status: authorized research direction; prototype in progress, NOT a protocol
freeze.** R5.2.2 remains the authoritative corrected post-B16 executable
boundary. B17 remains unexposed. No frozen requirement, oracle, checkpoint,
classification or application is amended by this decision.

## Decision

Stop the *exhaustive* R5.3 Python-test semantic reconstruction as a prerequisite
for B17–B20. Preserve all R5.3 source, worksheets, traces, inventories, tests,
unresolved diagnostics and progress records as prospective evidence. In
particular, the 187/21 unexplained candidate sites and 1,416/275 unchecked
semantic-event diagnostics in `R5_3-BULK-RECONCILIATION-WORKSHEET-PROGRESS.md`
are neither certified equivalent nor established defects. Do not claim a
zero-unexplained-difference certificate. The narrower claim sought here is
faithful frozen requirement intent and retention of the applicable *corrected*
R5.2.2 executable parent across affected carriers, not global equivalence of
all historically executed Python assertion paths.

Build a small versioned, backend-neutral semantic-requirement format: typed
scenario steps and observations with stable IDs, explicit achieved-history
applicability, original preconditions, and lineage. Prototype against the B01
intermediate-state correction, B11 deletion rule, B14 dependency failures,
and B16 users/ownership and migration. Historical requirements and the
R5.2.2 executable parent remain the authority for B01–B16; historical
semantic records are a *bounded bridge*, not retroactive amendments. For
B17–B20, author the semantic records before executable acceptance and before
either track is exposed; they become authoritative only after a separately
reviewed, independently validated protocol/case freeze.

## Gate before B17

1. Pin the effective loaded R5.2.2 parent, including B11/R4/R5 lineage and
   R5.2.1/R5.2.2 additive repairs, for both achieved histories B01–B16 and
   {B01,B04}. Do not rely on R5.2's `superseded_cases` dictionary alone.
2. Link frozen requirement clauses to semantic record IDs, identify the
   actual active carriers of each *changed* assertion, and retain every other
   observation, helper check, rejection path and precondition in any replaced
   method. Unselected parent methods continue to run unchanged. Fail closed on
   a missing carrier, conflicting edge, source drift or unknown applicability.
3. Specify B17 actor requirements (including `migrate`), role-bearing user
   results, permission/errors and no-write effects independently of track
   implementation. Derive backend-neutral external checks; review the records,
   carrier-retention inventory and executable cases independently before
   freezing them. An unsuccessful or blocked request activates no B17 skips.
4. Independently restore and revalidate both B16 checkpoints against the
   unchanged R5.2.2 parent and proposed composed suites, including corrected
   repair carriers. Pin inputs, outputs and loaded inventories in a separate
   freeze. Only then can B17 preflight, prediction, isolated execution and
   classification resume under a newly authorized protocol. Repeat the
   affected-carrier audit at B18–B20.

This replaces the *gate*, not the value of the preserved R5.3 evidence. If a
targeted audit finds a historical omission or cannot prove retained checks,
stop and adjudicate it; do not silently waive it as outside the bounded bridge.

## Methodological evaluation

Collect comparable evidence at each B17–B20 authoring/freeze: person/tool time
when available, semantic-record/fixture size, active methods inspected and
replaced, observations retained/changed/added, correction cycles, post-freeze
defects, and time to independently validated oracle. Compare with the recorded
B11–B16 workflow using explicit denominators and request-complexity caveats.
Whether semantic-first authoring is substantially simpler is a hypothesis,
not a result of this decision or of a passing format validator.
